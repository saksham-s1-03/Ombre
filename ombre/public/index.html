From bean to bar in four days

No shortcuts, no powders, nothing added that you can't pronounce.

  1. Roast

    Beans are roasted in small drums, just until the smell turns from green and sour to warm and nutty.

    Day 1

  2. Grind and conch

    Stone wheels grind the nibs for 48 hours, smoothing the texture and driving off harsh acidity.

    Day 1 to 3

  3. Temper

    We cool and re-warm the chocolate to line up its crystals. That is where the gloss and the snap come from.

    Day 3

  4. Mold and rest

    Bars cure for a day before wrapping, so the flavour settles before it reaches you.

    Day 4
